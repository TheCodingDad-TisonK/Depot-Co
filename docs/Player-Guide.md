# Player guide

## The ladder

You start in a shed and finish with a depot of five halls. Everything you do earns XP, every level opens something, and every fifth level the building itself grows overnight. The HUD under the top bar says what the next level opens; a card lists what the level you just reached brings, and every level pays a bonus of $100 times the level.

The building is never bought. When a level earns a bigger one the builders are booked, and the walls move at the next day roll (sleep on the cot, or let the clock run past midnight): a black fade, and you wake in the new building with everything you own where it belongs. A save from before 1.21 keeps the big hall it lived in.

## Your first day, in the shed

You start at 06:00 with $600, one rack row of one bay and a pallet jack. The intro in the bottom-left corner walks you through the first day; this is the same thing in full.

1. **07:30, the first truck.** Walk to the roll door **IN 1** on the west wall and open it: `E` on the door itself or on the console beside it. The truck backs in and the driver waits. It leaves at 11:30 whether you have emptied it or not, and anything still on it goes back unpaid.
2. **Sign.** With the door up the driver walks in and waits beside it. `E` on him signs the delivery note. Nothing comes off until you do.
3. **Unload.** Walk into the trailer. `E` on a pallet takes one box; carry it to the rack and `E` on a slot puts it there. Or grab the pallet jack from the south wall, `E` on a pallet lifts the whole thing, and `E` on the floor-level slot sets it in. You are paid $12 the moment a pallet is touched. The rack has one bay at level 1 and grows a bay a level until the hall; a slot holds up to twelve boxes of one line.
4. **The scanner.** `Tab` raises it. In the shed it has three pages: Home with the day and the alerts, Orders with every line and the slot to pick it from, and Picks. The other six open as you level. The wheel moves the cursor; `F` sets a waypoint on the highlighted row; rows marked ⏎ act on `Enter`.
5. **08:30, the first order.** It shows on the board by the door and on the scanner. The clipboard on the table is your office: `E` opens Depot OS with the orders, the shop, the crew and the stock.
6. **Pick.** `E` on the slot takes a box. Carry it to the **table** along the south wall and `E` puts it down. One box per trip until the cart at level 3.
7. **Pack.** With empty hands, `E` on the table packs the first whole order on it by hand: four seconds, and the parcel stands at the back of the table. The table holds eight boxes and six parcels. There are no short packs by hand.
8. **Ship.** Pick the parcel up and go out of the person door: the **van** parks at the shed front from 10:30 to 12:00 and 16:00 to 17:30. `E` at its back doors loads the parcel; it takes eight. It leaves on time, or `E` on the IN 1 console sends it early. You are paid when it goes. Nobody walks into the van.
9. **13:30, the second truck.** Same again. From 17:00 you can sleep on the cot in the corner, which skips to 06:00. The shed is rent free.

## The halls

**The small hall (level 5).** The staff door on the west wall opens into the lobby: the time clock, lockers, coat hooks and the notice board. Through the lobby door is the hall, 40 by 28: two rack rows of eight bays, IN 1 on the west wall and OUT 1 on the east, the pack line and bench on the east strip north of the office. The break room is the north-west corner (cot, coffee, vending machine, radio), the office the south-east corner, with the PC, the breaker and the control cabinet by its door. Everything ships by OUT 1 until the lanes come. Rent is $60 a day.

**The hall (level 10).** 60 by 48, five rack rows of fifteen bays, IN 2 and OUT 2 beside the first docks, and the three shipping lanes: **SEA** by OUT 1, **LAND** by OUT 2, **AIR** by OUT 3 once the deck stands. Through the strip curtain in the north wall is the production wing (see below), and the yard gains its car park, the road with its traffic and the neighbours over the fence. Rent is $110.

**The big hall (level 15).** The side walls move out to 72 m; the mezzanine and the sortation deck stand on the first morning, OUT 3 is cut and the deck accounts find you. **The annexes (level 20)** grow off the north wall: the returns hall east of the wing with a dock and a truck of its own, Hall 3 west of it with IN 3 and two rows of five. Rent is $160. **The far end (level 25)** adds Hall 4 behind the wing, through the wing's north wall.

## Doors, locks and the cabinet

The office, the break room, the staff entrance and the fire exit have doors. `E` opens or closes one, `Shift+E` locks or unlocks it. Staff carry keys, so a door swings open for them. The control cabinet beside the office door has a touch screen: hall lights, yard lights, every dock door, and **night mode**, which closes and locks everything at once. From level 7 a dock door left open or a person door unlocked at 23:00 costs stock.

## The pack line

From the small hall the table gives way to the bench and its pack line. Boxes go on the bench; pick an order on the bench terminal (or press `E` on the bench) and the line feeds its boxes onto the infeed belt; the case taper closes them into one parcel and the outfeed drops it onto the gravity shelf, where you or the packer pick it up for the truck. An order with at least half its boxes can be released short for 60% of the pay. The line jams occasionally: `E` on it clears the jam. Nothing moves in a power cut.

## The production wing

From the hall at level 10, through the strip curtain in the north wall. Order pallets of raw granulate on the office PC (Production app, $120 a pallet); they come with the next inbound truck. Bring one on the jack to the hopper and press `E` to tip it in (40 units). Start the moulding line on its screen or with `E` and pick a product: Depot Co. crates and storage bins from level 10, planters from 13. A box comes off every eight seconds and rides the main belt through the wall to the palletiser in the hall, which stacks eight to a pallet and drops the pallet beside it. Rack it like any delivery. Clients order your own goods at a better margin than anything a supplier sends.

## The baler and the wrapper

Every damaged box you bin and every order the pack line closes puts cardboard in the baler's chamber. At ten units, `E` on the baler (or BALE on its screen) presses a bale in eight seconds; outbound trucks take up to four bales away at $18 each. The stretch wrapper runs on a film roll good for twenty pallets; fit a new one on its screen for $30. Both stand from the small hall.

## Automation (shop)

The **shipping belt and dock loader** (level 9) take parcels off the pack line shelf, down the east wall into a shipping bay beside the loader, which pushes them into a docked truck with its door up. In the small hall the bay is at OUT 1; from the hall it is at OUT 2, and a sea parcel on that belt pays the forwarding fee until the deck takes over. The **AGV** (level 12) is a driverless pallet truck: set a pallet on its pickup square by receiving, or let the palletiser drop one, and it racks it and returns to its dock. The **gantry pickers** (level 14) put a crane on rails over every rack row you own, and a new row brings its own: each picks the boxes the bench still needs out of its row and sends them along the overhead pick belts, which merge above the east lane into one belt that ramps down to the bench. Any box or parcel riding a belt can be lifted off by hand. Every machine screen has a speed dial from 50 to 200 percent (the tune-up at 13 opens 250 and 300); the deck's dial reaches 300 on its own at 19. A fast pack line jams more often. All of them stop in a power cut.

## Drivers and the delivery note

When a truck docks the driver climbs down, walks along the trailer, climbs the dock steps and, once the dock door is up, comes inside and waits beside it with the paperwork. Nothing comes off an inbound truck until you press `E` on the driver and sign, or press SIGN on the dock console. Talk to them afterwards if you like. They get impatient after two hours. The van has no driver to sign with: it loads from the yard.

## The mezzanine and the sortation deck

Both come with the big hall at level 15. The **mezzanine** is a steel deck over the receiving strip, reached by the stair along the north wall. A goods lift stands against the west wall by IN 1: set a pallet in it with a jack or the forklift and it goes up by itself, rolls onto the feed belt and is racked in the upper row. The upper crane watches the orders and sends boxes the bench needs down a chute into the south pick belt. The crew never go up.

The **sortation deck** is where the lanes run themselves. Every parcel off the pack line shelf rides a spiral conveyor up beside the pack line and an overhead run north along the east lane onto the deck. A scanner arch reads its lane. The spine carries it west past three cells: the sea cell crates it, the land cell straps it, the air cell bags it. The collector takes it back east along the north wall and south to the spiral well, where the air gate and the sea gate drop air and sea parcels down spirals into the OUT 3 and OUT 1 loaders; land parcels ride on over the deck edge, down the east wall and down a third spiral into the OUT 2 loader. A parcel whose cell is full goes to the turntable at the end of the spine and round again. The deck opens **OUT 3** and the air clients, who pay half as much again, and it ends the forwarding fees. Every dock has a **shipping bay** beside its loader that holds nine parcels; the loader takes from its bay while a truck of its lane is docked with the door up. The **deck night shift** (shop, level 15) sorts everything on the shelf and the deck while you sleep. Three **deck accounts** (Meridian Exports by sea, Nordwind Parcels by air, Continental Retail by land) order from level 15: two to four lines, up to eight of each, at a third more.

## The annex halls

They come with the levels, never with money. Their doorways are cut and shuttered from the big hall on; `E` on a shutter says which level opens it. At level 20 the **returns hall** stands east of the production wing and **Hall 3** west of it with **IN 3** on its west wall, so a third inbound truck a day docks straight into the new rows. At level 25 **Hall 4** stands behind the wing, reached through the wing's north wall. The rows in Halls 3 and 4 are plain storage for the forklift, the AGV, the receivers and the pickers; the cranes stay over the main rows.

## Staff options and the plant page

On the Staff tab every worker has four options, each on the ladder: the **shift** (level 7) cycles day (08:00 to 18:00), early (06:00 to 16:00) and late (12:00 to 22:00), two hours more on overtime; a **training course** (level 9, $400) makes them walk a fifth faster and finish every task step a third sooner; a **raise** (level 11, $250) pays 10% more an hour and pins their punctuality at full for good; a **second role** (level 13, $350 once) lets them cover another job when their own queue is empty. The **Plant** app (level 10) lists every machine with its status, its speed dial and the button it has on its own screen, so the whole floor runs from the desk.

## The forklift battery and the wrapper

The forklift runs down while it drives, faster in third gear (Shift cycles creep, normal and fast), and charges only while its cable is plugged in at the charging point on the south wall: take the cable off the reel, walk it to the truck, `E` plugs it in. Flat, it crawls. A pallet on the forks that is not wrapped sheds a box on a fast corner: bring the pallet to the stretch wrapper on the jack and press `E`.

## Weather, seasons and Sundays

Four seasons of seven days each. Rain and storms soak the yard, snow settles in winter, and lightning is only noise. Sunday is closed: no trucks, no orders, a good day to sleep through. The radio in the break room has three stations; the vending machine sells a snack that makes you faster.

## Contracts, the bank and damaged goods

From level 5 a client offers a **contract** now and then: ship a number of their orders on time inside three days for a bonus, or pay a penalty. Contracts grow at 17, two run at once from 21, and from 24 a long one (five days, twice the bonus, a bigger penalty) comes now and then. The **bank** lends $5,000 at 1.5% a day from level 6; **theft insurance** (level 7) costs $40 a day and pays 80% of anything that walks off at night. A box dropped mid-air or shed off the forklift can be **damaged**: it will not go on a rack or the bench. Carry it to the red bin, and the client charges half its value.

The dock consoles, the bench terminal and the clipboard are touch screens: look at a button and press `E`.

## Build mode

`F2` is build mode, from level 5. Aim at any piece of furniture, a machine, a poster, a clock or a sign and `E` grabs it; it follows your aim along the floor (wall pieces slide along the nearest wall), `R` turns it a quarter, `E` puts it down, `Esc` drops it back where it was. `Backspace` puts a piece back where it started, `Del` removes it. `C` opens the catalogue: anything you removed can come back, and there are extras to buy, which sell back for half. The groups open with the ladder: the room, hall and wall pieces at 5, the yard at 19, the seating at 18 and the rest of the furniture at 23, conveyors at 17 and 18. The layout saves when you leave build mode.

## Looking at the crew

Look at a worker and the prompt gives their name, job, what they are doing right now and how they keep time. `E` on one who came in late has the word with them there and then. A receiver's pallet jack parks in a row by the west wall when they clock out, one slot a worker (by the player's own jack in the shed).

## Furniture and comfort

From the small hall the rooms are furnished: a TV on the break room wall, a dartboard, a microwave on the coffee counter, a standing fan in the office, rugs, a bookshelf, a printer, pictures. The catalogue sells the seating from level 18 and the rest from 23. Everything placed adds to the depot's **comfort**, shown on the Staff app, and comfort shortens the crew's breaks: half an hour with bare rooms, down to a quarter of an hour at comfort 10.

## Staff and the time clock

The crew come in from the yard through the staff door (the person door of the shed) and clock in at the reader beside it; from 18:00 (20:00 on overtime) they clock out and leave the same way. Pay at 06:00 is their clocked hours at the hourly rate, time and a half past ten hours. Punctuality is a trait: the reader screen shows who is in, who is late and who called in sick, and lets you put someone on overtime, give them tomorrow off, or have a word about lateness. Your own card works too.

A receiver pushes a pallet jack of their own. A picker with nothing to pick walks surplus boxes back from the bench to the racks.

## The bench, without a menu

A box on the bench is a thing you look at: `E` takes it back into your hand, or onto the cart, and the prompt says whether an open order wants it. The cart at the bench unloads only the boxes the open orders still want and, in the same press, takes the bench's surplus onto the cart to go back on the racks. An order only ever asks for stock that is on site and not already claimed by another order.

## Conveyors from parts

Build mode (`F2`), then `C`: the Conveyors group sells belts by the piece, straights of 2 and 4 m and quarter-turn curves from level 17, inclines up and down a metre and a 4 m high run hung from the roof from level 18. A carried piece snaps to the nearest free belt end, machine outlet, parcel shelf or inbound dock door, or to the nearest free belt start, machine inlet, outbound dock door or rack bay; when you drop it, it says what it takes from and what it feeds. A belt that ends at a rack bay puts its boxes on the rack. One that ends at an outbound door with a truck in loads the parcels. One that starts at an inbound door with a signed truck in takes the boxes off its pallets.

## Trouble

Trouble arrives one kind at a time.

- **Power cut** (from level 5): the dock doors, the PC, the coffee machine and new orders stop. Reset the breaker in the office, or wait 90 minutes for the grid.
- **The prowler** (from level 7): a dock door left open at 23:00 with no truck in it costs you a few boxes.
- **Inspection** (from level 8, every four to six days at 10:00): more than four things loose on the floor is a fine, and so is a dock door open with no truck in it. A clean floor earns reputation.
- **Cancelled orders**: an order left open thirty hours past its due time is cancelled by the client.

## Keys

`WASD` move · `Shift` run · `Space` jump · `E` use · `G` put down or let go · `Tab` scanner · `1`-`9` its pages, the wheel its cursor, `F` a waypoint, `Enter` acts on the selected row, `X` clears it · `F9` photo mode · `Esc` pause · `F3` FPS · `F12` screenshot · `F11` fullscreen · `Ctrl+Shift+D` links the dev console. Forklift: `W S` drive, `A D` steer, `R F` forks, `E` lift or set down, `G` get off.

## Returns

From level 8 an outbound truck now and then brings a parcel a customer sent back: unwanted, the wrong item, or damaged in transit. With empty hands, `E` in the trailer takes the return off. Carry it to the **returns desk** (west of the bench in the small hall, between the bench and the office from the hall on): `E` puts it on the desk (it holds four), `E` again starts the inspection, which runs by itself for a few seconds and needs power. The client pays $10 a return and $4 a box. The boxes come out on the desk's shelves: good ones go back on a rack, damaged ones go in the bin at no charge. A return left lying for a day costs a point of reputation. The packer fetches returns off the floor and the trucks and runs the desk when the bench has nothing for them.

**The returns hall (level 20).** Hall 2 is given over to returns: a **returns dock** of its own on the east wall, a **belt** from inside the dock door to an **intake**, three **inspection desks**, a **restock cage** against the far wall and a **compactor** for the damaged boxes. The returns truck docks at 09:30 and 15:00 with three to six returns out of everything you have shipped; open its door and, with the power on, the belt carries the returns off the trailer to the intake by itself. `E` on any desk inspects the next one in the queue (the queue holds eight). Good boxes land in the cage (it holds twenty-four). The desk by the bench goes with the hall.

## The map

Page 9 on the scanner (from level 5) draws the whole site from above at the scale that fits what you own: the hall with its rack rows (lettered), the docks in their lane colours, the rooms, the production wing, the annex halls (dashed until they come), the mezzanine. On it live: you, the crew, the forklift, the AGV, the trucks and your waypoint. North is up and the inbound docks are on the left.

## The day report

At every day roll the day that ended is closed off: money in and out, orders shipped and late, pallets received, boxes picked, returns inspected, the reputation change and the bank at the close. A card shows on the HUD for a while (any key closes it); the Stats app on the office PC keeps the last fortnight.

## Photo mode

`F9` frees the camera: `WASD` flies it, `Space` and `C` take it up and down, `Shift` is fast, the mouse looks, and the wheel zooms the lens from wide to long. The HUD goes and the world holds still while you line up the shot; `F12` takes it. `F9` or `Esc` puts you back where you stood.

## Settings

The pause menu (`Esc`) has the settings: mouse sensitivity and invert Y, the field of view, the head bob while walking, the quality, sound and volume, the film look, the FPS counter, and the guided intro. They belong to the machine, not the save. The pause menu also says when the game was last saved: it saves itself every half minute, when you pause, when you leave, and when the builders come in.

## Pallet jacks and empty pallets

The shed has one pallet jack; from the hall a second parks by the OUT docks (by the north wall once the sortation deck stands), and either one lifts a floor-level pallet. With an empty jack, E on the empty pallet stack takes a pallet off it; loose boxes of one line go on it by hand, and the loaded pallet stores on a rack like any other, or goes to the bin to write the whole load off.

The picking cart carries parcels as well as boxes, twelve items in all on three shelves. E with the cart at the parcel shelf loads a parcel, E in a docked outbound trailer unloads every parcel aboard, and G at the parked cart moves one item between the cart and your hands.

## The dev console

For testing. A separate little program: `npm run devconsole` opens it in a window of its own, with a live readout of the save and the cheats (money, levels, stage jumps, the clock, weather, trucks, orders, stock, crew, teleports). In the game, `Ctrl+Shift+D` links to it, or start the game with `npm run dev` to link from the first frame. The HUD shows LINKED while it is connected. Every command writes straight into the save.
